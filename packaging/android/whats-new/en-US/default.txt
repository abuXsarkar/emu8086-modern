First Android release of modern8086.

• Full editor, assembler, and 8086 emulator running locally on-device.
• Time-travel debugger with step-back, conditional breakpoints, and watch expressions.
• Eight live virtual peripherals, popped out as draggable panels.
• 10 hands-on tutorials, 13 UI languages.
• Source-compatible with classic emu8086 lab manuals.
